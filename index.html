01 · Research

Painting, trained, returned

Anna Katsur paints in oil and trains a neural network on her own canvases.

In Sensory Echoes each painting gets its own music and its own scent. The image goes into the machine and comes back into the room as sound, smell and surface. Music by Anna Katsur, with consultation from Max Viner.

Supported by Arts Council England through Developing Your Creative Practice.

1Paint

Oil on canvas.

2Train

A neural network learns her style.

3Translate

Each image becomes music and scent.

4Return

The work comes back to the room.
